---
name: why
description: Explain why you said or did one thing, or what a line means
argument-hint: "<the text you said, did, or wrote>"
disable-model-invocation: true
---

The text given is something you said or did in this session, or a line from a reply, a skill, or an instruction file. Change no files.

1. Find it. Locate the text and read the words around it.
2. Answer plainly, in one or two short sentences with no term the text used without defining it: what it means, and why you said or did it.
3. Source. Give what it rests on: a command and its output, `path:line`, or a doc read this session. If it was an inference or memory, say so, then check it against the source now.
4. Verdict. Say whether the reason holds. If it doesn't, or the text was vague or wrong, say what changes. If the text lives in a file, give the plain replacement and ask whether to make it.
